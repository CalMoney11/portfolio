Two hackathons, two apps

QuackHacks is the University of Oregon's hackathon. I've done it twice. Both times we went from an idea to a working demo in a weekend, using Git and quick prototypes to stay out of each other's way.

2025: assignment tracker

Canvas API, Google Auth, JavaScript, Git

Our team built a tracker that pulls your assignments from Canvas and lays them out on a calendar. You sign in with Google, and your due dates are all in one view instead of spread across course pages.

2024: photo recommendations

Image recognition, JavaScript

A web app that looks at a photo you upload, works out what's in it, and gives recommendations based on that content.

What I took away

  • Splitting work across a team with Git so nobody blocks anyone else.
  • Building the smallest version that demos well, then adding to it.
  • Explaining a technical project to judges who aren't engineers.
